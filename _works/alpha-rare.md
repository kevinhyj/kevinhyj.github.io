AlphaRare is an ongoing project on de novo protein design with non-canonical amino acids. It combines generator-predictor pipelines with experimental feedback to make rare-residue protein designs more controllable and testable.

## My Role

I used experimental data for DPO-based few-shot learning to improve design strategies, increasing enrichment of high-fitness proteins by 30%. I built generator-predictor pipelines using BoltzGen, Boltz2, and AlphaFold, yielding 2 successful designs and 14x activity improvement.
